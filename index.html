Research

Studies and methodology.

IC/IR alpha-mining engine

An open-source, headless fitness function for alpha signals. It scores cross-sectional Spearman rank IC, information ratio, and signal decay on Polars lazy evaluation. I validate it against planted signals, so I know the metrics respond to real structure and not to my own hope.

Polars · lazy evaluation · planted-signal validation

Five hypotheses, five nulls

I tested five XAUUSD microstructure hypotheses, session continuation, price-only key levels, time-series momentum, and candle-structure scans, against a sealed 2024 to 2026 window. All five returned nulls. That is the result I trust most. The window was sealed, nothing was peeked at in advance, and the methodology came before the answer. A clean null is worth more than a backtest that was allowed to cheat.

Sealed 2024–2026 window · no peeking · methodology first

Survivorship-bias-free equity factor model

An S&P 500+ universe reconstructed from index change history, so the factor model never sees a company that was not actually in the index at the time. Reconstructing membership is most of the work, and the whole pipeline is unit-tested end to end.

Reconstructed universe · point-in-time · fully unit-tested
